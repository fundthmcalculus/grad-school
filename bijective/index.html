Traditional head vs invertible meaning layer research preview

The same-size character-level TinyStories models (about 100K parameters, each trained for 30 million characters) side by side, all with the highest-likelihood character taken each step (no sampling). The traditional models score characters with the usual tied dot product (tied) or a distance likelihood on a plain codebook (identity control). The reversible models embed through an exactly invertible order-1 TSK map F: c routes the logic space back through F's inverse to score characters (its norm layer is lossy); ci does the same with an exactly invertible gain layer in place of that norm, so the whole chain embedding, logic layers, de-embedding is reversible by construction.
