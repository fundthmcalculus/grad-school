> Each class is held out of training in turn and treated as unseen; results averaged over held-out classes × seeds. The baselines' contamination is set to the complement rule's observed false-alarm rate, so the arms are compared at a matched operating point rather than at whatever default each ships with. 'Detection − false alarm' is Youden's J: higher is better, 0 means the detector is no better than flagging at random. Complement rule at θ=0.99, hamacher conorm. One-class SVM is trained on a random 20000-row subsample of each fold's training set (libsvm's O(n^2)-O(n^3) fit time makes the full ~86,181-row fold intractable at ten seeds x 12 held-out classes); the complement rule and Isolation Forest see the full training set.
