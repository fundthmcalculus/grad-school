Each threshold is the (1 − budget) quantile of the detector's scores on the benign-only validation split, so every row is selected without any positive label — which is the entire value of BETH shipping a benign validation split. Realized validation rates fall below their budget where scores are tied at the threshold, which is why the complement rows plateau and, at the tightest budget, collapse to zero detection while the surprisal holds: surprisal resolves 3,997 distinct scores; complement resolves 1,508 distinct scores.
