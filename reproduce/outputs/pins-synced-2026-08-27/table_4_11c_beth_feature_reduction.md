Selecting per arm would have made the curves incomparable. **One calibration for all four arms**: the threshold is the 0.990 quantile of each arm's own scores on the benign validation split. This deliberately differs from Table 4.11, which matched baselines by `contamination`/`nu` — that leans on each library's own threshold placement, and Isolation Forest's `contamination` sets the cut on the *training* score distribution, which is why its detection rate reads near zero there. ROC-AUC is threshold-free and identical under either convention. Inference time is the scoring of the full test split, timed separately from the fit because the two costs are paid at different times; throughput is rows/second at that measurement. **DO NOT COMPARE THE TRAIN-TIME COLUMN ACROSS ARMS IN THIS TABLE.** Each arm here trains on its own protocol's sample count — the fuzzy arms on all 763,144 rows, the one-class SVM on a 20,000-row subsample (libsvm is O(n²)–O(n³)), and Isolation Forest on 763,144 rows nominally but with `max_samples=256`, so each tree is built from 256.
