A fully fuzzy language model, under 100K parameters

Where the gap was

The original fuzzy models trailed neural ones by about 0.3 bits per character. Crossing every sequence mixer with both feed-forward layers shows the gap sat in one place: the zero-order, softmax-normalised TSK feed-forward layer. The fuzzy attention and recurrent mixers cost nothing.

d = 64, 2 layers, 10M characters, seed 0, best of lr 1e-2 / 3e-2. The TSK variants also carry about 25K more parameters (97K vs 73K).

How the feed-forward layer was fixed

Each step is still a fuzzy system: first-order TSK consequents (a rank-one linear function of the input, gated by the rule's membership), raw Gaussian firing instead of a softmax across rules, the HTSK exponent, and fewer antecedent dimensions. Then the mixer got more heads and the delta form.

All bars: 10M characters. Steps 1–3 are d = 64 with the accumulating fuzzy mixer; the last bars add 8 heads, the delta-rule form, short-conv 8 and d = 72. Single-seed except the 3-seed row and the final bar (2 seeds).

Heads did most of the mixer work

With the new feed-forward layer fixed, going from 1 to 8 heads (each with its own 16-rule base) lowered loss by 0.11 bits per character. Everything after that was worth a few hundredths.

Accumulating fuzzy mixer, d = 64, 10M characters, mean of 2 seeds. 16 heads were attempted but stalled the GPU (VRAM spill) and were not measured.
