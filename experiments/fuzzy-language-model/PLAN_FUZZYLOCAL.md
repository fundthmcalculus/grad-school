# PLAN_FUZZYLOCAL: toward all-fuzzy blocks (registered 2026-10-19, before running; new direction from the user: "move away from convolutions and layer operations like that to proper fuzzy inference layers")

**What counts as a fuzzy inference layer here.** Fuzzification (membership functions on the inputs), a rule base (antecedents combined by a t-norm, consequents), aggregation and defuzzification. The model's two TSK components already are that: the feed-forward (Gaussian rules over a projected antecedent, first-order consequents) and the memory mixer (fuzzy partition of key/query space, delta-rule consequents). What is not fuzzy inference today, per block: (1) the causal depthwise convolution; (2) RMSNorm (before the mixer, before the FFN, and at the output); (3) the linear q/k/v and output projections of the mixer; (4) the linear antecedent projection and the slope of the FFN; (5) the sigmoid gates alpha (forgetting) and beta (write strength); (6) the tied embedding / output matrix.

**Stage 1 (this plan): replace the convolution with fuzzy local rules.** The convolution is a learned linear smoother over the last 8 tokens. The fuzzy counterpart already in the code is the multi-tap antecedent of the layer-0 FFN (`--ante-taps k`, `TapLinear`): a rule's antecedent is a conjunction over tokens t-k+1..t ("IF the previous token is A AND this one is B"), which is what a trigram rule needs and the convolution cannot express (it only blends).
Arms (BPE3072 tiered recipe, 30M characters, seeds 0-4; baseline = the recipe with the convolution, mean 1.0547, 98,528 params): `noconv` (97,760); `noconv_t3` (99,296); `noconv_t4` (FFN x1.45); `noconv_t8` (FFN x1.2); all at or under 99,999 parameters.
**Predictions.** Dropping the convolution without a replacement costs 0.005-0.030 BPC (70%). `noconv_t3` recovers at least half of that loss (45%) and matches or beats the convolution baseline (25%); more taps are better up to 4 (50%) and `noconv_t8` is worse than `noconv_t4` because the FFN had to shrink (60%). A fuzzy local rule layer replaces the convolution at no cost (within 0.003 of the baseline): 25%.
**Decision rule.** Paired over five seeds. A replacement within 0.003 BPC of the convolution baseline, or better, is adopted and Stage 2 starts; a loss of 0.003-0.010 is a price to weigh against "all-fuzzy"; more than 0.010 means the convolution is doing something the rule layer cannot (report what).

## Next stages (not run; for the owner to steer)
- **Stage 2, normalisation:** RMSNorm replaced by a fuzzy-partition normalisation (memberships summed to one) or removed; first test: remove each RMSNorm in turn (is it needed?), then the fuzzy alternative.
- **Stage 3, projections:** the mixer's linear q/k/v/out maps replaced by rule-based maps (a small TSK system per head), or the antecedent made to read the raw residual stream through per-dimension membership functions (fuzzification proper) instead of a learned linear projection.

## Stages 2 and 4 registered before running (`flm/fuzzy_parts.py`, `flm/run_allfuzzy.py`, `flm/run_fuzzy_stage.py`, `v45-fuzzystage`; goal text set by the owner 2026-10-19: replace the convolution, RMSNorm, linear projections and gates with fuzzy systems and report each replacement's cost or gain)
Base: the BPE3072 tiered recipe with the convolution kept (98,528 params, mean 1.0547 at 30M, seeds 0-4); each arm changes ONE component (the stages compose later).
* **fnorm** (stage 2): every RMSNorm (two per block and the output norm) becomes a first-order TSK gain controller on z = log rms(x): five Gaussian membership functions, consequents b_r + k_r (z - c_r); initialised to be exactly RMSNorm (checked: max difference 5e-7), 98,628 params. **scaleonly**: normalisation removed, only the per-dimension weight kept (ablation: is normalisation needed at all?).
* **fgate** (stage 4): the two sigmoid gates of every memory mixer (forgetting alpha, write strength beta: `nn.Linear(d, 8)`) become zero-order TSK systems: six Gaussian rules over the full 48-dimensional layer input (no learned projection), consequent logits per head, defuzzified by the weighted average; 99,456 params. **fnorm_fgate**: both (99,556).
Predictions: fnorm within 0.003 BPC of the baseline (55%), better by >= 0.003 (15%), worse by >= 0.003 (30%): the gain curve starts as RMSNorm and has few extra parameters. scaleonly worse than the baseline by >= 0.02 (70%) or diverging on some seed (25%). fgate worse by 0.003-0.020 (50%), within 0.003 (25%), better (10%), worse by more than 0.020 (15%): the linear gate sees every input dimension with its own weight, the rule gate only through distances to six centres. fnorm_fgate approximately the sum of the two single effects (60%).
Order of runs: Stage 1 (`v44-fuzzylocal`) first, then these, one GPU cell at a time; no run longer than 300M characters.
