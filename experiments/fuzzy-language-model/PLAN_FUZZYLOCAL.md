# PLAN_FUZZYLOCAL: toward all-fuzzy blocks (registered 2026-10-19, before running; new direction from the user: "move away from convolutions and layer operations like that to proper fuzzy inference layers")

**What counts as a fuzzy inference layer here.** Fuzzification (membership functions on the inputs), a rule base (antecedents combined by a t-norm, consequents), aggregation and defuzzification. The model's two TSK components already are that: the feed-forward (Gaussian rules over a projected antecedent, first-order consequents) and the memory mixer (fuzzy partition of key/query space, delta-rule consequents). What is not fuzzy inference today, per block: (1) the causal depthwise convolution; (2) RMSNorm (before the mixer, before the FFN, and at the output); (3) the linear q/k/v and output projections of the mixer; (4) the linear antecedent projection and the slope of the FFN; (5) the sigmoid gates alpha (forgetting) and beta (write strength); (6) the tied embedding / output matrix.

**Stage 1 (this plan): replace the convolution with fuzzy local rules.** The convolution is a learned linear smoother over the last 8 tokens. The fuzzy counterpart already in the code is the multi-tap antecedent of the layer-0 FFN (`--ante-taps k`, `TapLinear`): a rule's antecedent is a conjunction over tokens t-k+1..t ("IF the previous token is A AND this one is B"), which is what a trigram rule needs and the convolution cannot express (it only blends).
Arms (BPE3072 tiered recipe, 30M characters, seeds 0-4; baseline = the recipe with the convolution, mean 1.0547, 98,528 params): `noconv` (97,760); `noconv_t3` (99,296); `noconv_t4` (FFN x1.45); `noconv_t8` (FFN x1.2); all at or under 99,999 parameters.
**Predictions.** Dropping the convolution without a replacement costs 0.005-0.030 BPC (70%). `noconv_t3` recovers at least half of that loss (45%) and matches or beats the convolution baseline (25%); more taps are better up to 4 (50%) and `noconv_t8` is worse than `noconv_t4` because the FFN had to shrink (60%). A fuzzy local rule layer replaces the convolution at no cost (within 0.003 of the baseline): 25%.
**Decision rule.** Paired over five seeds. A replacement within 0.003 BPC of the convolution baseline, or better, is adopted and Stage 2 starts; a loss of 0.003-0.010 is a price to weigh against "all-fuzzy"; more than 0.010 means the convolution is doing something the rule layer cannot (report what).

## Next stages (not run; for the owner to steer)
- **Stage 2, normalisation:** RMSNorm replaced by a fuzzy-partition normalisation (memberships summed to one) or removed; first test: remove each RMSNorm in turn (is it needed?), then the fuzzy alternative.
- **Stage 3, projections:** the mixer's linear q/k/v/out maps replaced by rule-based maps (a small TSK system per head), or the antecedent made to read the raw residual stream through per-dimension membership functions (fuzzification proper) instead of a learned linear projection.
- **Stage 4, gates:** alpha and beta as fuzzy systems of the input (the delta rule's forgetting and write strength as rule outputs).
Each stage is a 30M-character five-seed screen (about an hour), a 300M confirmation (12 minutes a seed) and a blind coherence set on the 300M checkpoints. **No 1B-character (or 3B) runs: the owner dropped them on 2026-10-19; the 300M cell is the largest budget from here on.** The deferred 3B-character tiered run and the 1B-character run of any new recipe are cancelled. ## Stage 5 registered before running: fuzzifying the tokenizer and the token / output tables (`flm/fuzzy_tokens.py`, `flm/prep_bpe_dropout.py`, `flm/run_fuzzy_tokens.py`, `v51-fuzzytokens`)

## OPEN, for further exploration (owner note 2026-10-20): how to fuzzify the head of the embedding table
**What failed.** `emb_fall` (stage 5, and `e2e_all`) made each of the 384 most frequent tokens a softmax membership vector over 48 prototypes (embedding = weighted average of the prototypes; tied output = weighted average of the prototype scores). It cost +0.130 BPC alone and +0.277 on top of the fuzzy blocks, against +0.040 for the same treatment of the 2,688 tail tokens over 12 prototypes. The head carries most of the text, so its quality dominates.
**Why it probably fails.** A softmax row is a point on a simplex: non-negative, summing to one, so each token sits inside the convex hull of K prototypes and cannot use sign or scale freely; with K = d = 48 the hull is full-dimensional but the parameterisation is badly conditioned (the logits are a ratio code), and a crisp token is a free vector in R^48.
**Ideas to try (each is a 30M-character, five-seed screen against the recipe, params kept under 99,999):**
1. *More prototypes*: K = 96 or 128 for the head (needs a K x d prototype matrix; trim the FFN to pay).
2. *Signed or unnormalised memberships*: Gaussian firings without the partition of unity (as the FFN rules use), or tanh-signed memberships, so the embedding is a free linear combination of prototypes rather than a convex one.
3. *Fuzzy residual*: embedding = crisp low-rank part + fuzzy membership part (a small free correction on top), so the fuzzy table carries structure and the crisp part carries what the simplex cannot.
4. *Frequency split*: keep the very top tokens crisp (the few hundred that cover most characters) and fuzzify only the middle band; measure the cost per frequency band first (per-token loss by band for `e2e_all` vs the recipe) to see where the loss comes from.
5. *Fuzzy only the output side* (keep crisp input rows; defuzzify the scores through prototypes) and, separately, *only the input side*: the loss may come from one half of the tie.
6. *Prototypes from a trained crisp table*: cluster the trained head embeddings (fuzzy c-means) and initialise memberships and prototypes from the clusters, then fine-tune; this separates "is the form expressive enough" from "can training find it".
7. *Sharper memberships*: a learned temperature per token or sparsemax, to move from a soft average toward few-rule firing.
**Diagnostics first (cheap, no training):** per-frequency-band loss and per-token loss difference of `e2e_all` vs the recipe on the validation set; coverage of the top-k tokens (what share of characters the 128 / 384 most frequent tokens spell); the effective rank of the trained crisp head table.
The tail table (tail tokens over 12 prototypes) is the first thing to retry with the same ideas: its +0.040 may shrink under ideas 2 and 3.
