Detection scales with model capability

The interpretable, unsupervised TribbleFIS one-class detector (log-domain trimmed score, benign-only fit) tested across nine models from 50M to 3B parameters. Two questions: does the strict 1%-false-positive gate improve with scale, and when do internal activations beat reading the prompt's surface?

1. Deployable detection vs model size

Detection rate at a 1% benign false-positive rate — the deployment metric. It climbs with capability: unusable at 50M, a usable triage gate (≥0.5) from ~0.4B, best at 3B. Hollow points are base (non-instruction-tuned) models; filled are instruction-tuned.

2. When do activations beat the surface baseline?

Within-length AUROC of the activation monitor minus the surface-only (token-statistics) baseline. Above zero, watching the model's internals wins; below, just reading the prompt wins. On the easy corpus most models win; on the hard corpus (safeguard, lexically-distinct injections) only the 3B model crosses over — capability widens where the method applies.
