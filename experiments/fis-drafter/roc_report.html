Operating points — FIS vs Mahalanobis

Detection at two fixed benign false-positive rates, and the ranking metric (AUROC). The two detectors match at 5% FPR; at the strict 1% FPR the fuzzy score's heavy benign tail costs it.

Construction & inference performance

The FIS detector is effectively free to build and run. Construction is dominated by the shared per-layer feature transform; the fuzzy fit itself is microseconds, and scoring is ~10 µs per prompt — negligible against a model forward pass.
